Work with your other characters

PlanWonk knows your other characters on the same realm and faction, from the last time you played each: what they hold and what their plans need. It uses that to save you buying, selling or crafting twice.

What you’ll see

Section titled “What you’ll see”
  • Spare items: a step that needs something another character has spare says so: Ivo has 40 spare: mail it.
  • Their needs: an item another character’s plan needs isn’t sold here. Its tooltip says Brin’s plan needs 12: mail it to Brin.
  • Mail on its way: mail you send to one of your characters counts as theirs before they open it.

Ask another character to craft gear

Section titled “Ask another character to craft gear”
  1. Open the Gear tab and find Crafted by your characters.
  2. Click the item. The crafter’s plan now shows For Dax: … with the materials.
  3. Log in as the crafter, make it, and mail it. The request clears once it arrives.

Ask another character to craft a material

Section titled “Ask another character to craft a material”
  1. Right-click a step that buys or gathers it, for example Buy 3× Silver Rod.
  2. Pick Ask Brin to craft 3× Silver Rod, or the same with you mail the mats to send the materials yourself.
  3. Brin crafts it and mails it. Your plan stops buying it, and tells you when to collect it from the mail.

Not sure which is cheaper? Pick Compare the costs: buy or ask in the same menu.

Plan a character on its own

Section titled “Plan a character on its own”

To play a character without help from the others, turn on Plan this character on its own in Settings (/wonk plan plan solo on). Its plan then never counts on another character: no spare items to mail it, no gear or crafts from them, no disenchanting by them and no asking them to craft. The others can still use what it has: its spare items and crafts stay in their plans.

At the mailbox

Section titled “At the mailbox”

On the Send Mail tab, a button such as Fill: 2× Silver Bar to Brin fills in the mail for you. You click Send yourself.

Craft with a friend

Section titled “Craft with a friend”

A duo partner is another player who also runs PlanWonk.

  1. In a party of two, right-click Share recipes with Mira? in the plan. Nothing is shared until you do.
  2. Once Mira shares hers too, a step for something she can make offers Ask Mira to craft ….
  3. Mira picks Accept or Decline. Each of you mails your own part.

Either of you can cancel; the other is told. It’s for two people: running it between your own two game windows is multi-boxing, which isn’t allowed.

Details

Section titled “Details”
  • Other realms and factions are left out of everything that goes by mail. Only the Coin tab’s account wealth lists them, with a total for each faction.
  • Tooltips: Held: 10 on 3 characters (Shift for who and where); Brin can craft this: right-click its step to ask. A character whose profession window was never opened can’t be asked: open it once on that character.
  • Mail on its way: what you buy on the AH counts in your mailbox too, until you open it.
  • Where a request sits in the crafter’s plan: an item the asker can wear now comes first; one they can’t wear yet (Dax asked for it · needs level 20) goes where it costs the crafter least. Make now puts it first; Make it where it costs least puts it back.
  • Requests by command: /wonk plan plan request list, /wonk plan plan request cancel 1, /wonk plan plan request cancel all. Bind-on-pickup items are only offered to their crafter.
  • Mailing the mats: your plan shows Mail 2× Silver Bar to Brin; Brin’s line says Dax mails 2× Silver Bar, then collect 2× Silver Bar from the mail. Right-click the mail button to pick another mail when more than one waits; each click after the first attaches one stack.
  • Compare the costs prints each way’s cost in chat at what your time is worth, cheapest first: the AH’s price and trip, or the crafter’s whole job (two character swaps, its mats and trips, the in-between crafts, a recipe to learn first, the craft, the mail back), for example Brin’s job: swaps 2 min, its mats 40c, make 2× Bolt of Linen Cloth 10 sec, craft 3× 15 sec, mail 30c 2 min. Swaps and cast times are guesses (1 minute a swap), and a line says which. A partner’s reply time isn’t counted.
  • Partners: /wonk plan partner share Mira shares recipes; the setting Share recipes with my party (off by default) does it for you when a party of two forms. Always accept Dax’s requests puts them straight into the plan (/wonk plan partner accept Dax ask turns it off). Mail between accounts is assumed to take an hour. PlanWonk remembers partners, their recipes and how each answers (Mira: answers 3/4 · last heard 2 days ago, 3 in 4 assumed at first), until Forget Dax or /wonk plan partner forget Dax.
  • Not seen in play yet: all of this page. The partner messages are tested, not yet sent between two players in game.
