Steps

A step is its action, then its facts in grey, each joined by “ · “. Some examples are shortened.

When you take it, PlanWonk puts a star raid marker on them (the first time they’re the quest window’s NPC, your target, under the mouse or on a nameplate) and says in chat: Escort: The Defias Traitor · marked: star · /focus while targeting them. It can’t set your focus for you. If you abandon the quest or it fails, the star comes off. Not seen in play yet

Every step says why it’s there, when you hover it: Skill up: 70% · Uses your Coarse Thread and Light Leather, To pay for: Train Journeyman Skinning. The line is a few facts joined by “ · “: Skill up: 70% is the share of crafts that give a skill point.

A step to gather something says how to gather all of it: one need is one step, with the beasts or plants for the whole count. For something you can skin, pick or mine, the better way is the step and the other is the grey line under it: farming with or buy on the AH: ~9s 20c under it, or the AH buy with or skin ~23 beasts · ~32 min · Darkshore (how many, the time, then the place). A drop a mob has is the same: or kill ~96 Ornery Plainstrider · ~48 min · Barrens under its buy. A drop or a fish the plan can’t weigh that way is a Gather step, with the AH as its grey line. Its other grey line names one more way to get it, where the plan can price one: or fish: The Zoram Strand, Ashenvale · 25%, or from: Sand Crawler, Westfall · 43%, or skin: …, or gather: …, or open: … (the chance is per catch, kill, beast, node or container). Of the ways, it names the one with the best value for its time, the trip included, that you can do now; hover it for the time. Fishing’s time is a guess (15 s a cast).

A drop that comes less than a quarter of the time is luck, worded as such: watch for Dreadmaw Crocolisk (18%).

Objectives done together

Section titled “Objectives done together”

When the route does several quest objectives at one place, they are one step. It names the place, then what to do there, each action once: Around Echo Isles, Durotar: kill 8 Hexed Troll, 8 Voodoo Troll; collect 4 Durotar Tiger Fur, 1 Zalazane’s Head. The line under it names the quests: For: Practical Prey, Zalazane.

  • The place is the subzone. Where there is none, it’s the coordinates: Around (57, 13), Barrens.
  • The counts are what your quest log still wants. A part you finish leaves the step; the step is done when all its parts are.
  • The arrow points at the nearest mobs or objects the step still needs, and moves on as parts are done.
  • An item’s name comes from your quest log. For a quest you haven’t picked up yet, the step names the mob it drops from: collect 15 from Kolkar Wrangler.
  • The menu on the step skips each of its quests by name.

There is no set distance for “one place”. The route puts objectives in one step when it does them together: back to back on the same ground, or when the kills for one finish the other. A rare drop, a place to find and a pick-up or turn-in between them keep their own steps.

Objectives left for later

Section titled “Objectives left for later”

An objective you’ve begun, whose step isn’t the next one, says why on its line: how far it is, where the rest comes from, and what leaving it saves against doing it now: Fel Moss 3/8 · finish later with Grellkin · ~41 s saved. “With” names the mobs whose kills the route counts on for the rest (another stop’s, or those on the way), else the objective’s own. The saving shows only when doing it now would take longer. On a step of several objectives, this line takes the place of For: ….

Accepting and handing in

Section titled “Accepting and handing in”

With Accept and hand in the plan’s quests on (the default), a quest the plan has a step for is accepted when its window opens, completed, and handed in. A quest in your log that’s complete is handed in too, unless you hold it for your group. When an NPC opens a menu first (several quests, or other things to talk about), PlanWonk picks the quest from it: a turn-in first, then a quest the plan has a step for. Where the reward is a choice, PlanWonk takes the one the gear upgrades picked (the step’s Pick: …); with no pick it leaves the window open for you. Each action says one line in chat:

  • PlanWonk: accepted Webwood Venom
  • PlanWonk: handed in Iverron’s Antidote · took Barkmail Vest (+7% kills)
  • PlanWonk: Iverron’s Antidote: pick a reward

Hold Shift as the quest window opens to leave that one to you. Turn it off with /wonk plan settings autoquest off or in the settings window.

Built and tested offline, not yet seen in play.
