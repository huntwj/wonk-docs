Tooltips

To check in game

Item and mob tooltips are checked in game. The lines marked * are built and tested, not yet seen in play.

Items

Section titled “Items”

Each line leads with what to do:

Line Meaning
Use 37 * A plan uses this many
Need 37 more * A plan needs more than you have
Save 20 for Enchanting * Kept for a profession you’ll learn later
Sell 40 on the AH, ~20s * Sell this many on the AH, for about this much
Sell 26 to a vendor, ~2s 60c * Sell this many to a vendor
Quest: … * A quest in your log or route wants it, though the game doesn’t mark it a quest item
Brin’s plan needs 12: mail it to Brin * Another of your characters needs it
Held: 10 on 3 characters What you and your same-realm, same-faction characters hold (Shift: who and where). With one character: Held: 8, all yours; with none, when a plan wants it: Held: none on any character
Best: disenchant ~45s (Brin, after 30c postage) · AH ~30s expected · vendor 12s * For an item no plan uses: disenchant, auction or vendor, side by side. The AH’s number is what listing it is worth on average: the price after the AH’s 5% cut, less the risk it doesn’t sell. disenchant ~3c+ means some of the dust or essences aren’t priced yet, so it’s at least this. A thin AH price says so: AH ~30s expected, only 1 listed. Hold Shift for the disenchant’s mats: Disenchant: 1.9 Strange Dust ~4s, … An item the game never lets you disenchant (an enchanter’s own wands, PvP rank gear) has no disenchant here
AH ~1s 15c each · seen 2 days ago * On dust, essences and shards: their AH price. With MarketWonk installed, that addon shows the price and this line is left out (a thin one: AH ~1s 15c each · 1 listed · seen 2 days ago; one over 7 days old says its age once: seen 9 days ago). No line until an AH scan has priced it
Duplicate: 1 worn · 1 spare * You’re wearing this and hold another copy in your bags or bank. When the spare is an upgrade for one of your characters, the next line says Spare: mail it to Brin (upgrade); then Best: says what the spare is worth
A planned upgrade: now, at level 24 * A weapon among your planned gear upgrades, and when
+18% upgrade, Worn (Leveling set), … * The gear line: see Upgrade your gear
Upgrade for +12% Brin · +5% Ann (at 22) * It would be an upgrade for these characters
AH ~1s 15c each · 7s 20c here * At a vendor, when one on the AH, with its margin (10%, or 25% for a weak price), costs less than the vendor’s price
Dax asked for this: mail it to Dax * A craft request (Ivo is making this for you, on its way from Ivo for the asker): see Ask another character to craft

Gear you’re wearing or have in an equipment set, and items another character asked you to craft, get no sell advice.

Hold Shift: every option

Section titled “Hold Shift: every option”

To check in game

Built and tested, not yet seen in play.

Without Shift the tooltip has one Best: line. Hold Shift on any item, one the plan uses too, and every option follows, one line each, the best first:

Line Meaning
You: upgrade +12%, Han: upgrade +7% It’s an upgrade for you, or for another of your characters
Keep: 3 for the plan A plan uses this many (Keep: later crafts use it for your own recipes’ mats)
AH: 1g now · 98s usual · net 95s · expected 76s (80% sells, a guess) The cheapest listing in the last scan, the steady price, what a sale pays after the AH’s 5% cut, and what listing it is worth on average. The 80% chance that a listing sells is a guess, and says so, until it’s measured
Disenchant: ~38s (Sela) What its dust and essences sell for, and who can disenchant it (a + after the amount: some aren’t priced yet)
Vendor: 5s What a vendor pays

The upgrade and keep lines come first; the ways to sell follow, the most coin first. Then the disenchant’s mats, and who holds the item.

With MarketWonk installed, its lines come first (the market’s now, usual and net prices) and PlanWonk’s follow a blank line. The AH line is then shorter: AH: net 95s · expected 76s (80% sells, a guess).

Bag icons

Section titled “Bag icons”

To check in game

Built and tested, not yet seen in play: which bag frames Forever’s client draws, and how the marks look.

Each item in your bags gets a small mark in its top-left corner for its best action, the same as its tooltip says:

Mark Meaning
Green arrow An upgrade over what you wear, for one of your rated specs
Shirt Saved in one of your equipment sets
Lock Kept: a plan uses it or needs more, a quest wants it, or a later craft or profession does
Dust Best disenchanted
Hammer Best sold on the AH
Coins Best sold to a vendor

No mark: nothing to do with it (worth nothing), or its info isn’t loaded yet. Turn them off with /wonk plan settings bagicons off, or in the settings window’s Bags section.

Mobs

Section titled “Mobs”
  • What the quests in your log want from it: Hidden Enemies: drops it (~18%).
  • For every mob in the zone, what a kill is worth: ~1s 16c a kill. Hold Shift for what that’s made of: coin, drops with their chance, skins.
