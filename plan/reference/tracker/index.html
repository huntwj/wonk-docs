The window

To check in game

The Guide and Plan switch and the Guide view’s goals with their steps are built and tested, not yet seen in play. So are the Professions tab’s goals, the goal after each of Next’s steps, the Leveling tab’s Show menu, the Orgrimmar: N things line, the Report button, Update gear and the account’s wealth. So are the Guide’s live lines and then: line, the Goals tab, the “you are here” marks, Start in, Hints in the Guide, the Zen view and the first-run card.

PlanWonk has one small window, the tracker, meant to stay on screen: full, minimized to its title bar (which then shows the next step), or hidden. The minimap button: see Settings and the minimap button.

Three views

Section titled “Three views”

The title bar has a switch, Guide, Zen and Plan. The window shows one view at a time. The Guide tells you where to go and what to do. Plan shows how it’s worked out and lets you change it. Both are the same plan. The first time you open the window, a card at the top says so; Got it puts it away for all your characters.

View What it shows
Guide Where to go and what to do now: the current goal with its steps, the live lines under the top card, and the goals after it on one line
Zen Easy farms for what your characters need, and the farm you’re on (Farm in the Zen view)
Plan How it’s worked out, and your settings for it: every goal and step, on the tabs below
  • The window opens in the view the setting Start in names (Guide by default; /wonk plan settings start guide|plan). The switch changes it as you play.
  • /wonk plan guide opens the window in the Guide view.
  • The views read the same plan, and share the window’s place, size and lock.
  • Minimized, the title bar shows the next step in any view.

The Guide view was a window of its own before. If you had it open, the window now opens in the Guide view; where you had put the guide window, and its lock, are no longer kept.

The Guide view

Section titled “The Guide view”

The plan is shown as goals, each with the steps it takes (/wonk plan guide 1, 2, 3… sets how many goals):

  • A profession goal is a craft and the skill it reaches: Leatherworking to 89 · craft ~23 Embossed Leather Gloves. Its steps are what the craft needs (gather, buy, learn), then the craft.
  • A gathering goal is a stretch of a gathering profession: Skinning to 93 · skin ~29 beasts.
  • A leveling goal is a level and the town you quest out of: Level 14 · The Crossroads, a slim heading over its cards (below).

After a goal’s name comes its progress, 2/5: steps done of steps in all.

For a character with a leveling route (Night Elf for now), the first line names the route’s next hub and the level to expect there: Next: Auberdine at 12 (to check in game).

  • The current goal is open. A step you’ve done is ticked and stays until the goal is done; the step to do now is marked and larger; the rest are plain. Steps or quests done one after another are one ticked line with their count, ✓ 9 done, so the step to do now stays in sight (to check in game).
  • Under the step to do now: its details, why or how long, and the other way to do it (or buy on the AH: ~9s 20c).
  • Each line is a verb, a count and what it’s of: Kill 4 Strigid Owl, Collect 15 Gnarlpine Mane, Use 4 Relics of Wakening. A drop is named by the item once the quest is in your log; before that, by its mobs (Collect 2 from Elder Nightsaber). A drop’s chance a kill follows it: (31%).
  • Where is in a step’s tooltip: the quest it’s for, the NPC or place with its zone and map coordinates, and the way there for a far step. The arrow and the map pin point the way. A step with no Where is done where you are (to check in game).
  • Later goals are one line: then: Leatherworking to 106 · +57 more. /wonk plan guide 3 names two of them there (to check in game). The Plan view’s Goals tab lists them all with their progress.
  • The live lines, under the top card (to check in game): the Coin line when you’re short within the next two steps, and the step it’s before; the as you go line, what to gather on the way; up to three blue hints (the Goals tab used to show these; Hints in the Guide turns them off: /wonk plan settings hints off); turn-ins you’re holding for your group (a click releases one); and crafts you owe your other characters (right-click to cancel).
  • A leveling goal’s steps are cards, one for each place, in the order you do them (to check in game). A town’s pick-ups, turn-ins, training and vendor are one card; a camp’s kills, drops and things to use are another. The top card is where you are now; each later card says how long it takes (~6 min). Each step is a line with its count at the right (0/4, from your quest log). Pick-ups back to back are one line (Pick up 3: A, B, C), and a quest’s things to use at one ground are one (Use 4 Relics of Wakening). A line you finish is ticked and stays where it is; the card goes once all its lines are done. A long card shows 6 lines, then +N more. The arrow points at the top card’s nearest line still to do.
  • If you see: under the top card, up to 3 things worth a stop if they show up (to check in game): a rare drop (Gnarlpine Shaman · The Sleeping Druid · 15% a kill), mobs on the way that may drop what a quest needs (Grell · Fel Moss · 40%), a wandering vendor the plan buys from, while you’re in a zone he walks. One shows only when what it brings, for the time it takes, beats what questing brings an hour. A rare drop is here, not a card’s line: it’s luck, not a stop. Click × to ignore one for this character. Each with a place is a small dot on the minimap. (Gathering nodes and quest givers you pass aren’t here yet.)
  • A rare in view goes first in If you see: (to check in game): Mother Fang · Rare elite 10 · ~2 min, when its kill’s coin, loot and XP, for the walk and the fight, beat what questing brings an hour. Chat says Rare: Mother Fang · Rare elite 10 once, and again only after 5 minutes out of view. × ignores that rare for this character (and in SilverDragon, if you have it); /wonk plan rares unignore brings it back.
  • Group: under an elite step (to check in game): Group: Lord Melenas · ~10 min · nearby: Kaelis (Priest 9), Tov (Warrior 8). The minutes are how long a group takes to find, from how often the chat asks for one and the players near. Click a name to open a whisper with Care to group for Twisted Hatred? I’m a lvl 8 Warrior written in; nothing is sent until you press Enter (or, with /wonk plan settings whisper send on, the click sends it: one whisper a click). A name you asked for a quest, or who said no, isn’t offered again this session. Right-click a name for its menu; right-click Group: to search the Group Finder, count players here with /who, or count guild members online.
  • Right-click a line for its menu, the same as in the Plan view: point the arrow, skip the quest, hold a turn-in, Sold out, Couldn’t find <name>. Nothing happens until you pick an entry. A line of several pick-ups lists each quest’s entries by name (to check in game).
  • The top card’s buttons: at a trainer, Train (what the plan wants here, as the trainer window’s own button); at a vendor, Buy; on a quest’s page, Accept; on its progress page, Turn in. Each click does one thing. Away from those windows there are no buttons: the arrow points the way. A line you click holds the arrow until its card is done (to check in game).
  • The steps you see hold still unless a new order saves enough (to check in game). What’s enough depends on what it would change:
    • the card you’re on: the most. By default a new order must save about twice the time the card takes. Set it in seconds or minutes, or never until done, with Keep my current step unless a new one saves (/wonk plan guide hold now 2m);
    • the cards you see without scrolling: by default a new order must save about 1.4 times the time they take. Set it with Keep what’s next unless a new order saves (/wonk plan guide hold next 60);
    • the cards you scroll to: about 0.7 times the time they take. Past what the Guide shows, the order moves freely. When a faster order is held back but saves more than that last amount, a line says so: Faster order available · saves ~3 min, with a Switch button. Nothing changes until you click it. The Plan view keeps its own rule.
  • A step two goals use belongs to the first and says so (also: Fine Leather Belt). The later goal shows it as Light Leather (with Leatherworking to 89), and it’s counted once.
  • A coin step (Sell …, or a farm or quests for the coin) comes just before the step it pays for.
  • The step the arrow points at is highlighted. Click a step to point the arrow at it; click again to let go. When the arrow points at a step that isn’t shown, a line under the goals says where and why.
  • Why a step is next is in its tooltip, or under the step to do now with /wonk plan guide why.
  • The buttons above the goals (Leveling, each profession) leave that kind of goal out. The plan keeps it.

The Plan view’s tabs

Section titled “The Plan view’s tabs”
Tab What it shows
Goals Every goal in order with its progress, the Guide’s marked; your goals, dungeons near your level, professions planned for later, and Settings
Leveling Every leveling step. Its Show menu ticks dungeons to get ready for; untick All quests to plan only their quests, with no grinds (Get ready for a dungeon)
Professions Every step of each profession, under its goals, and those planned for later. The buttons along the top show or hide a profession in this tab only; a merged pair (Tailoring + Enchanting) is one button
Coin Coin in hand and in the mail, what’s in your auctions, what the plan costs, each shortfall and the step it’s before, your goals, and your account’s wealth
Gear Upgrades in your bags and Update gear, the weapon path, and what your characters can craft

The tab you pick is remembered for each character.

You are here: on every tab, the goal and the step the Guide is on are marked with the Guide’s own arrow, and a marked step’s tooltip says You are here. Right-click a step for its menu; where the plan has a Why this? for the step’s decision, it’s there.

Goals on the Professions tab

Section titled “Goals on the Professions tab”

Each profession’s steps are grouped under goals, as in the Guide view:

  • A goal’s line ends with its progress. The first goal is open; the rest are shut.
  • Click a goal to show or hide its steps. Your choice is kept for each character.
  • A step you’ve done stays under its goal, ticked, until the goal is done.
  • A step two goals use says also: Fine Leather Belt; the later goal lists it as Light Leather (with Leatherworking to 89).

The Goals tab, from the top

Section titled “The Goals tab, from the top”

It was the Now tab. What it showed live (the next steps, as you go, the hints, held turn-ins and requests) is in the Guide’s live lines now.

  1. The Coin line, only when you’re short within the next two steps. It shows on every Plan tab.
  2. Every goal in the plan’s order, with its progress: 2. Leatherworking to 106 · craft ~31 Fine Leather Belt 0/3. The Guide’s current goal is marked, with the step it’s on under it (now: Buy 46× Coarse Thread).
  3. A dungeon focus, when the Leveling tab’s Show has one: Leveling: only Wailing Caverns.
  4. Your goals: the ones you set, all of them; right-click one to remove it.
  5. Dungeons near your level: click one to make its quests a goal.
  6. Professions planned for later, with their stockpile.
  7. Settings: a click opens the settings window.

The blue hints are the Guide’s. Among them: mail to collect or about to expire, skinning or herbs here, the richest mob around, junk worth selling now (Bags: 2 free · sell junk at Jark: saves ~29s: the loot its slots save before your next free sell, less the trip; to check in game), a better bag, rare drops to watch for, a key and the chest it opens, upgrades in your bags, items to post before logging out, ammo or drink to top up.

Controls

Section titled “Controls”
  • Click a step: point the arrow there and pin it on the map. A few lines act on a click instead, and say so: a held turn-in releases it, a dungeon near you becomes a goal, an item under Crafted by your characters opens your profession window at it (your own craft) or asks its crafter, and [Update gear: equip N, save <set>] equips and saves.
  • Right-click a step: its menu (skip, Too hard on a quest, hold a turn-in, cancel a request, remove a goal, point the arrow). Nothing happens until you pick one.
  • Hover a step: why it’s next, and where.
  • Hold Shift: a line under a step that was cut short (it ends in …) shows in full, wrapping, for as long as you hold it. Holding Shift does nothing else, and a Shift-click is a click. Hovering a cut line shows all of it too. (To check in game.)
  • The corner grip sets the width and how tall it grows before scrolling.
  • The lock button stops it moving or resizing, in both views.
  • The note button: report a bug or suggest an idea.

A timed quest’s steps count down (20 min left), and its pick-up shows the limit (timed: 45 min). Drop chances on step lines are coloured: green at 50% a kill or better, yellow from 25%, red below.
