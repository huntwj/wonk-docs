Deals

Buy what’s listed under its usual price, only the units that make a profit.

  1. Open the AH and the panel’s Deals tab. A row reads Buy 18 Silk Cloth: 47s 83c · usual 80s · +4g 89s net · <1 day.
  2. Click Buy 18 ≤ 74s 99c. It checks the AH’s prices now.
  3. Click Buy 14: the units still at or under 74s 99c.
  4. Click Pay 6g 72s to buy them.

A row with Open instead of Buy needs a closer look: click it, or any row, for its card with every number behind the deal.

Details

Section titled “Details”

A row

Section titled “A row”

Buy 18 Silk Cloth: 47s 83c · usual 80s · +4g 89s net · <1 day: how many pay, what they cost each on average, the usual price, the profit on all of them, and how long they take to sell here. Rows show the item’s icon and name, with the numbers on a second line. Hover a row for the game’s item tooltip; shift-click links the item in chat; the mouse wheel scrolls the panel.

While it checks your prices the tab says Deals: 3 (checking the prices). With no prices at this house yet it says Deals: none yet: prices come from Check prices or a full scan, and deals from a few days of visits. While deals rest on few days of prices, it says Prices still settling: 4 of these on 2 days of data.

The Buy button

Section titled “The Buy button”
  • Buy ≤ 74s 99c (or Buy 18 ≤ 74s 99c when it fits): its click searches the item now. It buys at most 18, never a unit over 74s 99c, the cap.
  • Checking…: open the item’s row in the AH’s results; its prices come then.
  • Buy 14: the units at or under the cap now. None ≤ 74s 99c when none are left.
  • Pay 6g 72s: the AH’s quote, to confirm. If any unit costs more than the cap, the total is more than those units cost in the fresh search, or it’s more coin than you have, the button says Not bought and offers no confirm.

Every word the Buy button can show is in shopping.

Only deals that pass every check have Buy on their row. Every other row says Open, and its card says which check it fails: the profit within the usual price’s ±, an old listing, slow sales, or no sales seen.

The card

Section titled “The card”

Click a row to open its card; < Back to deals returns. Its Find and Buy are on its first line. One card, line by line:

  • Silk Cloth · Horde · searched 12 min ago, ~85% still up;
  • Reset the market to [box] [Model’s price]: the price to reset the market to. The box starts at the model’s best price to post again; type another and press Enter, or click Model’s price to put it back (/fmarket reset 4s 50c does the same, /fmarket reset default puts it back);
  • Buy 18 at 45s-52s each · 8g 61s in all: the units that pay and their prices, then (+40 more at 78s and up: not a deal) for the dearer ones;
  • the reset: Reset to 80s: buy 58 listed below it · 32g 10s in all · 55s 34c each on average (45s to 79s), then the cheapest left (Then the cheapest left: 12 at 81s), how long it takes to sell, what it nets posted again at that price (Relisted at 80s: ~74s after the cut and the deposit at risk · +10g 82s on the whole reset), and, when your coin is short, With 20g you can buy 40 (19g 60s) and reach 62s. With nothing under that price: Nothing listed under 80s: the market’s already there. The Buy button’s tooltip says the same;
  • Sells for ~80s each (72s-88s) · 30 a day sell here · 12 days of prices;
  • +4g 89s net on 18 · +27s 17c each · 57% on what you spend, and its sum: 80s usual - 4s cut (5%) - 1s deposit at risk - 47s 83c paid = +27s 17c each;
  • Profit each 27s 17c, usual price ±3s: more than the ±: whether the profit is bigger than the usual price’s own uncertainty, or within the ±, so a guess;
  • ~50% gone a day at 72s-88s (sold or cancelled; ±8%) · 41 units watched a day over 9 days: of the units listed in the usual price’s range, the share that went each day. A scan can’t tell a sale from a cancel, so this is the most that sold. Before the add-ons have watched an item for a while it says not watched yet;
  • In one 24 h auction: ~18 of 18 expected to sell · +4g 89s against the whole cost;
  • ~14 h to sell 18 at 30 a day here;
  • Cap: 74s 99c each, the most a unit can cost and still pay (each resells for ~75s after the cut and the deposit at risk);
  • Quick Buy: every check passes, so the row has a Buy button, or No quick Buy: and the check it fails;
  • the risks that apply: an old price, slow sales, a house’s higher cut.

Under usual

Section titled “Under usual”

Under usual below the deals lists items listed under their usual low, by more than the price’s own ±. They’re cheap, but not always a profit after the cut. Their Buy is capped under the usual low too.

Settings

Section titled “Settings”
  • /fmarket set dealMinMargin 15%: the least a deal must make, as a share of its cost (default 10%). Type the %: a bare 15 means 1500%.
  • /fmarket set dealMinProfit 5000: the least it must make, in copper (default 5000, which is 50s). It can be a price formula, like max(50s, usual * 0.2): each deal works it out on its own numbers, and its card shows the sum. A deal it can’t be worked out for isn’t shown, and the tab says how many: 1 deal not shown: your least-profit formula needs usual.
  • dealMaxCost (Most a deal may cost): by default your spare coin (less what ForeverPlan sets aside). A deal is cut to what you can afford; the tab counts those you can’t: 2 deals need more coin than you have to spare.
  • /fmarket set dealsFromWeak true: show deals whose profit each is within the usual price’s ±.
  • /fmarket set dealsFromListings true: show deals whose usual price rests on listings, with no sales seen.

All of them: settings.
