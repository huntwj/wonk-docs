Selling

Sell what’s in your bags: post or vendor, how many, and at what price.

  1. Put the item in the AH’s Sell tab. The panel’s Selling tab shows what to do: 20× Silk Cloth: vendor 4c each · post 48c 24h, ~40% sells · worth 30c each → post, and how many: Post 4 now · up to 5 sell a day at 48c · 16 kept in bags.
  2. Click Use 48c 24h to fill in the price and duration, then the AH’s own Post.

Or post straight from your bags: under To post, click Post 4 on a row.

Undercut? Under Undercut, click Cancel on a row, then post it again from To post.

To see what sold and what you made, open the Ledger tab.

Details

Section titled “Details”

The advice

Section titled “The advice”

20× Linen Cloth: vendor 12c each · post 30c 24h, ~40% sells · worth 9c each → vendor

  • vendor 12c each: what a vendor pays.
  • post 30c 24h: the best price and duration to post at.
  • ~40% sells: of what you post, the share expected to sell before the auction runs out.
  • worth 9c each: what posting is worth per item, after the AH’s cut, the deposit you may lose, and your time.
  • → vendor: the better of the two. A vendor pick offers no Use.

The advice also shows on the scan strip at the panel’s foot. Search the item first for sharper advice: a search sees every price, not only the cheapest.

Post 4 now keeps what you list within what sells in one auction, so you don’t list against yourself; the rest is kept in bags. While no sales have been seen at that price, the line says Sales a day at 48c: not known yet (searches from now on measure it). The Posting setting (postPace) switches from what sells in one auction to everything in your bags.

The Selling tab’s lists

Section titled “The Selling tab’s lists”

Rows show the item’s icon and name, with the numbers on a second line. Hover a row for the game’s item tooltip and the words in it; shift-click links the item in chat; the mouse wheel scrolls the panel.

  • To post: 3: what in your bags is worth posting rather than vendoring, best first: Post 4 Silk Cloth at 48c · 24 h · up to 5 sell a day · 12 kept in bags. Post 4 posts those 4 at that price for 24 h. Gear posts one piece a click. While sales a day aren’t known yet, the row says sales a day not known yet and offers 1. Items not priced yet are one line: 2 items not priced yet (hover or search them).
  • Undercut: 2: your auctions with cheaper units listed ahead: 20× Silk Cloth at 48c: 14 cheaper listed ahead · ~40c each if left up · ~43c each if reposted. Cancel shows only when reposting at today’s best price is worth more than leaving it up, after the new deposit (the one you paid is gone either way). It cancels that auction; you post it again from To post.
  • On the AH: 6: your auctions up now, each with its buyout and time left. Refresh asks the AH for them again.

Each button is one call to the AH, and only from your click. If the row changed under the pointer (a bag sorted, a price moved), the click does nothing and the list is drawn again.

The words you may see

Section titled “The words you may see”
  • (guess): too little has been seen of this item yet, so the chance leans on a starting rule.
  • (queue unknown: 10-90%): no full list of this item’s prices was seen, so how many are cheaper than yours isn’t known. The real chance is somewhere in that range. A search of the item settles it.
  • no lower: 1s at least: no post under 1s is suggested (the postFloor setting). Very cheap posts are known to jam the AH. no lower: a vendor pays more and no lower: well under usual are the other floors.
  • the model would post at 38s; your floor … holds it at 41s · ~25% sells there: your floor or ceiling (postFloor, postCeiling, each coin or a price formula) moved the post from the model’s best price. Your limit wins; the line says what it costs.
  • No post offered: your floor needs usual: your floor or ceiling is a formula that needs a price not seen yet for this item, so no post is offered on a guess.
  • (cut not known): this house’s cut isn’t known yet, so the net is a guess.

The ledger

Section titled “The ledger”

The Ledger tab, and /wmarket ledger in chat:

  • Mailbox: 1g 20s · 2 sold: coin from sales waiting in your mail, after the cut, with the deposits paid back.
  • On the AH: 6 · 3g at buyout: your auctions up now, at their buyout.
  • Last 7 days: sold 4 2g · bought 2 80s · unsold 1, then a line each, newest first: Sold 20× Silk Cloth: 9s 50c net · profit 1s 20c · 2 h ago. profit shows where this character bought the item: the sale’s net over what it paid. Unsold came back by mail, and the AH kept its deposit.

How many lines it shows follows your chat frame, or the chatLines setting.

The report

Section titled “The report”

Under those lines, the Ledger tab adds up the week by item, as many rows as fit; the last says +5 more (/wmarket report). /wmarket report prints it all in chat, and /wmarket report 30 covers the last 30 days.

  • Last 7 days by item: net +12g 40s · +1g 77s a day: the week’s net, and that over its days. When the ledger began within the period, it says so and divides by the days it covers.
  • Best day: 2 days ago +8g · worst: the last 24 h -1g: the best and worst 24 hours of the period.
  • A line an item, best first: Silk Cloth: sold 40 for 32g, bought 60 for 21g, 2s deposits lost · +10g 98s. Sold is after the AH’s cut; deposits lost are those the AH kept on what came back unsold. An item sold and never bought in the ledger (bought before it began, farmed or crafted) says what you paid isn’t known, so its net is the sales side only; one bought before the period says bought before these 7 days. A return whose deposit the ledger didn’t keep says (deposit not known), and the net leaves it out.
  • Held too long: what came back unsold since it last sold, with the count and the chance it sells within a day at the price asked: Silk Cloth: came back unsold 3 times since it last sold · 12% chance it sells within a day at 80c each. Auctions up now that are more likely to come back than sell are listed too, with the chance they sell in the time they have left.

The report only reads what the ledger already kept: it never opens, takes or deletes mail, and asks the AH nothing.
