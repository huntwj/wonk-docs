Settings

MarketWonk

Section titled “MarketWonk”

Change one with /wmarket set <name> <value>; /wmarket set <name> default puts it back; /wmarket set lists them all with what each means. The four money settings (dealMinProfit, dealMaxCost, postFloor, postCeiling) take coin (50s, 1g 20s) or a price formula, like max(50s, usual * 0.2), worked out for each item.

Setting Default What it changes
dealMinProfit 50s The least a deal must make: coin, or a formula
dealMaxCost spare The most a deal may cost: coin, or a formula. spare is your coin less what PlanWonk sets aside for your plan. A deal is cut to what you can afford, and one you can’t afford is left out
dealMinMargin 10% The least a deal must make, as a share of what it costs. 15% and 0.15 both work; a bare 15 is refused (“did you mean 15%?”)
dealsFromWeak false Show deals whose profit each is within the usual price’s own ±
dealsFromListings false Show deals priced from listings alone, with no sales seen
postFloor 1s Never suggest or queue a post under this: coin, or a formula; never under 1s
postCeiling none Never suggest or queue a post above this: coin or a formula; none for no ceiling
postPace drip How much the post queue offers: drip, what sells in one auction at the price (the rest stay in your bags), or all, everything in your bags
shoppingTo faction Where a new shopping item goes: faction, your faction’s list (shared by its characters), or character, your own. Both lists show
checkMax 0 How many items Check prices searches at most, best first. 0: all of them
chatLines 0 How many rows /wmarket deals and /wmarket ledger print, and the panel’s Ledger tab shows. 0: what your chat frame shows
panel true Whether the panel beside the AH is shown. /wmarket panel switches it
bar true Whether the scan strip at the panel’s foot is shown. /wmarket bar switches it
borrowed true For an item MarketWonk has no price for yet, show Auctionator’s or TradeSkillMaster’s, marked whose (“Auctionator: ~12s each · 3 days old”). A borrowed price is never used for deals or posting. All your characters
besideOthers both Only with Auctionator or TradeSkillMaster loaded: both shows every line of ours too; theirs leaves the AH now line to them, and keeps usual, net and the rest

A true-or-false setting takes true or false (on and off work too). A bare whole number in a money setting is copper (5000 is 50s).

All characters, or this one. The settings window (/wmarket settings) has tabs, as the game’s macro window does:

  • All characters: the value your characters share. A change here reaches every character that hasn’t set its own. A row says how many have their own, and Change them too gives them the shared value.
  • This character (its name): a dimmed row uses the shared value. Use your own gives this character its own, to change; Use the shared value goes back.
  • Your Alliance or Horde characters: a third tab, shown once you play both factions, since their markets differ. A value here is for that faction’s characters.

A choice is a button: each click moves it to the next choice. A box takes what /wmarket set takes; Enter or a click away applies it, and Escape puts the shown value back. A value that isn’t kept says why under its box, and the old value stays. Under a money setting’s box you see what it comes to on an item: the last one you hovered, or one you type in Work formulas out on at the foot. A change applies at once.

Hover a setting to see every level’s value, the one in use first. Set per character or faction lists every value a character or faction has of its own, by name, each with Use the shared value, and Clear all for this character.

The four settings you’d change most (least profit, most cost, the post floor, posting) come first; More settings opens the rest. When Auctionator or TSM also shows prices is there only with one of them loaded. The chat rows, the panel and the strip have no faction tab, and borrowed prices have one value for all your characters. /wmarket set changes this character’s value, and /wmarket set <name> default clears it, so it uses the shared one again.

MarketWonkData

Section titled “MarketWonkData”

These size what MarketWonkData keeps in the game’s saved file. The defaults are measured to keep the file quick to load; change them only to troubleshoot. /wmd set lists them, /wmd set <name> <number> changes one, and /wmd set <name> default puts it back.

Setting Default What it changes
maxDayRecords 150000 Days of prices kept in all, over every item; the oldest go first, at login
ledgerRecords 2000 Sales and purchases kept per character
rawDays 3 Days of raw prices kept when you don’t run the price archive
priceDays 30 Days a single price seen is kept (each item keeps its newest)
warnFresh 15000 Raw prices kept before /wmd warns that the file is growing
warnUnarchivedDays 7 With the archive in use, days of prices not archived before /wmd warns
