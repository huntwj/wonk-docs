What the Auction House add-ons do

On every item tooltip

Section titled “On every item tooltip”

A few lines under the item, at any time:

  • AH now: 45s each · 2 h ago · 70% up · 120 listed: the cheapest listing when you last searched it, how long ago, how much of that listing is likely still there, and how many were listed.
  • AH usual: 48s each (40s-55s) · 30/day · 12 days: what it usually sells for at your home AH, the range, how many sell a day, and how many days of prices that rests on.
  • Net: 45s 60c each (5% cut): what a sale at that price puts in your mail, after the AH’s cut.
  • Buy 5 here: ~2g 10s in all (42s each): what the stack in your hand would cost to buy at today’s listings.

At another house, the home lines say (home) and a second AH now (Blackwater): … line gives the price where you stand. Every line is in tooltips.

At the AH

Section titled “At the AH”

The panel opens beside the AH window, with five tabs:

  • Deals: items listed well under their usual price, each with a card that spells out the buy.
  • Search: recipes at this AH by the level of what they make, and who on your account can learn them.
  • Shopping: your shopping list, priced at today’s listings.
  • Selling: for the item in the Sell tab, whether to post it or sell it to a vendor, at what price and for how long; what in your bags is worth posting now; and your auctions that are undercut.
  • Ledger: coin waiting in the mail, your auctions up now, and the last week’s sales and purchases.

The scan strip at the panel’s foot offers Check 12 prices for the items in your bags and your own auctions, and Full scan for the whole house.

Away from the AH

Section titled “Away from the AH”

The AH overview (/wmarket houses, or the minimap’s add-on button) shows every AH you can reach, how fresh its prices are, and your auctions there.

What they never do

Section titled “What they never do”
  • Buy, post, cancel or search on their own: every one of those is your click, one call a click.
  • Take or delete mail.
  • Send anything anywhere: the prices stay in your saved files.
